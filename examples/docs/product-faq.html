Tidewater S2 Sensor FAQ

Sample content for the RAG Starter Kit demo. Fernhill Instruments and the Tidewater S2 are fictional.

Hardware

What does the Tidewater S2 measure?

The Tidewater S2 measures water temperature, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, pH and turbidity. A pressure sensor also reports water level with an accuracy of plus or minus two millimetres.

How long does the battery last?

With the default 15-minute sampling interval the lithium battery pack lasts about 20 months. Sampling every minute reduces battery life to roughly four months.

Is the sensor waterproof?

Yes. The housing is rated IP68 and has been tested to a depth of 50 metres for 30 days.

Maintenance

How often does the sensor need calibration?

We recommend calibrating the pH and dissolved oxygen probes every 6 months. Conductivity and temperature probes only need a yearly check.

  • Use the Fernhill calibration kit CK-4 for pH buffers.
  • Rinse probes with distilled water before storage.

What does the warranty cover?

Every Tidewater S2 comes with a three-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Damage from ice, vandalism or opening the sealed housing is not covered.

Data and connectivity

How does the sensor send data?

The S2 uploads readings over LTE-M or NB-IoT. Where there is no cellular coverage, a LoRaWAN module can be fitted instead.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Readings can be downloaded as CSV from the Fernhill Portal or pulled through the REST API. Raw data is kept in the portal for five years.
