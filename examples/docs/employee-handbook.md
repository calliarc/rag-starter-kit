# Fernhill Instruments Employee Handbook

> Sample content for the RAG Starter Kit demo. Fernhill Instruments is a fictional company.

Fernhill Instruments designs field sensors for monitoring rivers, reservoirs and coastal water. This
handbook explains how we work together and what you can expect from the company.

## Working hours and remote work

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you can
arrange your day as you like, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

Fernhill is a hybrid company. Engineers and support staff may work remotely up to three days per week.
Lab technicians work on site because the calibration benches cannot leave the building.

### Home office stipend

Every employee who works remotely at least one day per week receives a one-time home office stipend of
600 EUR for a desk, chair or monitor. Receipts must be submitted through the expense tool.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 26 days of paid vacation per calendar year, plus public holidays of the
country they are employed in. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount.

Up to five unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on
31 March. Vacation requests of more than ten consecutive days should be submitted at least four weeks
in advance.

### Sick leave

Tell your manager as early as possible when you are sick. A doctor's note is only required after three
consecutive sick days. Sick days do not reduce your vacation balance.

### Parental leave

Fernhill offers 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave to every new parent, regardless of gender, for
births and adoptions. Leave can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year.

## Travel and expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase. Claims older than 90 days cannot be reimbursed.

The daily meal allowance during business travel is 55 EUR. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Book economy
class for flights shorter than six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

## Equipment

New employees choose between a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every
three years. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT help desk within 24 hours.
